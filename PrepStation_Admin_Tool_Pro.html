AI STRATA DOCTOR DeepSeek & LLM Divergence Diagnostic & Auto-Repair Engine

Pinpoints and fixes EVERY syntax error AND structural divergence from AI question generators (DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Perplexity):
• UTF-8 Byte Order Marks (BOM) that break JSON.parse
• Missing "subjects" wrapper (direct questions array placed on bundle)
• Full-text answers instead of option letters ("A", "B", "C", "D")
• DeepSeek search citations (e.g. [citation:1], [citation:19])
• Strata ID collisions (e.g. Stage 3 mislabeled as strata_2)
• Non-sequential bundle lock states (enforces Bundle 1 unlocked, Bundle 2-N locked)
• Unescaped LaTeX backslashes (\sqrt, \frac) and trailing commas.
